# Golden Corpus v1

Golden Corpus v1 contains 21 governed awkward cases: seven retained private
report documents, seven accepted hard-gold cases and fourteen diagnostic/review
cases (some cases serve both document and downstream evaluation strata). It
covers digital and mixed layouts, tables, charts, OCR routing, financial
periods/signs/comparability, fundraising boundaries, identity ambiguity,
coverage, website failure, sparse evidence, legacy preservation and Viewer
rendering. The canonical machine-readable definition is
`golden/corpus-v1.json`.

The corpus separates syntax recovery from semantics. Its EJA targets test 33
P&L rows, 32 financial-position rows and the 50/31/9/10 allocation. Ambiguous
marketing/fundraising and additivity cases are review targets only: no corpus
output is treated as a new public fundraising estimate or human gold.
