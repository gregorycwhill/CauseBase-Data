# CauseBase Principles and Guardrails

**Status:** Canonical shared CauseBase product contract  
**Version:** 0.1-draft

1. Describe before evaluating. CauseBase reports organisations and evidence; it does not rate worthiness, quality or donation suitability.
2. Prefer observable activities to organisational rhetoric. Retain organisation self-description separately and attribute it.
3. Evidence precedes synthesis. Deterministic acquisition and extraction narrow the evidence before LLM interpretation.
4. Preserve provenance appropriate to the field. Explainable and contestable does not require a microscopic universal claim graph.
5. Distinguish authoritative fact, self-report, independent evidence, community input, deterministic derivation, heuristic estimate, LLM interpretation and imputation.
6. Required estimates never masquerade as direct measurements. Method, components, evidence and uncertainty remain visible.
7. Taxonomy pluralism is foundational. No CauseBase or external taxonomy is universal by default.
8. Human-readable fields and machine-readable embeddings complement one another.
9. The card is the knowledge object; projections are generated, not independently authored.
10. Search and semantic proximity are navigation, not recommendation.
11. Accepted corrections change governed evidence/inputs and regenerate dependent outputs.
12. Public contestability applies to moderated proposal and decision records, not automatically to raw submissions.
13. Charities may correct facts and provide self-description; they do not obtain editorial control over CauseBase synthesis.
14. Stable understanding and transient current activity have different evidence and freshness cycles.
15. Version consequential data, methods, taxonomies, prompts and models.
16. Open CauseBase-originated output does not override upstream source rights.
17. Keep Viewer static and thin unless a demonstrated requirement cannot be met through public artefacts.
18. Treat agents as first-class consumers: selective retrieval, stable IDs, provenance and freshness must be usable without corpus-scale context.
19. Reproducibility supports legitimacy: evidence → processing → CauseBase understanding → release.
20. CauseBase should remain inspectable, challengeable and replaceable.
